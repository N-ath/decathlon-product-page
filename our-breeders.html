Our breeder charter

Home-raised

Puppies grow up indoors with their mother, never in cages or farms.

Health-tested parents

Hips, elbows, eyes and breed-specific DNA tests before every litter.

Limited litters

No more than one litter per mother per year, and a maximum of four in total.

Early enrichment

Daily handling, sounds, surfaces and outdoor time from 3 weeks.
