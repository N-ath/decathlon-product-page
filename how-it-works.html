How it works

A puppy is a 12-to-15-year commitment. Our process takes the time to make sure it's the right match, for you and for the dog.

  1. Choose a puppy

    Browse available puppies and read the breed guide. Each profile shows age, health and temperament.

  2. Reserve online

    A refundable 200 € deposit holds your puppy for 14 days. Nobody else can reserve it meanwhile.

  3. Short interview

    A 20-minute call with a canine coach about your home, schedule and plans with your dog.

  4. Meet in person

    Visit the breeder or a partner store. Meet the puppy and, when possible, its mother.

  5. Welcome home

    Pay the balance, sign the adoption contract and leave with the health booklet and starter kit.

Minimum 8 weeks

Puppies never leave their mother before 8 weeks of age.

No shipping

Every puppy is handed over in person. We never ship animals.

Rehoming promise

If life changes, we help find your dog a new home. It never ends up in a shelter.

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the price?

First vaccinations, microchip and registration, deworming, a vet health check, a 12-month health guarantee for hereditary conditions and a starter kit (harness, leash, bowls, toy).

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes. The 200 € deposit is fully refunded if you cancel before pick-up, for any reason. It is deducted from the final price otherwise.

Can you deliver the puppy to my home?

No. We believe meeting in person matters, and transport is stressful for young puppies. Pick-up is at the breeder's home or at a partner store.

When can my puppy start running with me?

Short, playful walks are fine from the start. Wait until growth plates close (around 12 months for medium breeds, 18 months for large ones) before canicross or long runs. Ask your vet.

Do you check who adopts?

Yes. Every adopter has a short interview with a canine coach. We may suggest another breed if the match doesn't look right.
