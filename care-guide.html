Puppy care guide

Everything you need for the first months, from the first night to the first trail.

Settling in

  • A quiet cornerSet up the bed in a calm spot, close to where you sleep for the first nights.
  • RoutineSame times for meals, naps and toilet breaks. Puppies sleep 18 to 20 hours a day.
  • Gentle introductionsKeep visitors to a minimum and let your puppy come to people on its own terms.

Socialise & learn

  • Vaccination boostersBooster at 12 weeks, then rabies as required locally. Ask your vet.
  • Meet the worldNew sounds, surfaces, people and calm dogs, a little every day.
  • Basic cuesName, sit, recall. Short 5-minute sessions with rewards work best.

Building up

  • WalksRule of thumb: 5 minutes of walking per month of age, twice a day.
  • No running yetCanicross and long runs wait until growth is complete (12 to 18 months).
  • SwimmingA great low-impact sport. Start in shallow, calm water with a dog life jacket.
